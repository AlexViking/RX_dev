# Video Hero Production Guide

## Overview
Create two cinematic videos (morning and evening) showing a modern house exterior transitioning to interior view through a window.

---

## Video Specifications

### Technical Requirements
- **Duration**: 8-12 seconds
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum, 4K preferred
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps or 60fps
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264) + WebM (VP9) for browser compatibility
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9
- **File Size**: < 10MB per video (optimized for web)
- **Audio**: None (muted videos)

### Export Settings
- **Bitrate**: 5-8 Mbps for 1080p, 15-20 Mbps for 4K
- **Codec**: H.264 (High Profile) for MP4
- **Color Space**: sRGB
- **Compression**: Medium-High quality

## Video 1: Morning Scene (Light Mode)

### Scene Description
**Setting**: Bright sunny morning, natural daylight

### Camera Movement Timeline

#### 0:00 - 0:03 | Opening Shot
- **View**: Exterior of modern luxury house
- **Architecture**: Clean lines, large windows, minimalist design
- **Lighting**: Warm morning sunlight (golden hour ~8-9 AM)
- **Details to Show**:
  - White/cream exterior walls
  - Large floor-to-ceiling windows
  - Modern landscaping (green grass, minimal plants)
  - Clear blue sky
  - Shadows showing morning sun angle

#### 0:03 - 0:06 | Camera Move Toward Window
- **Movement**: Smooth dolly/crane move toward main window
- **Speed**: Slow, cinematic (ease in/out)
- **Focus**: Keep window in center frame
- **Lighting**: Sunlight reflecting on glass
- **Details**:
  - Window frame becomes more prominent
  - Reflections fade as camera approaches
  - Begin to see interior through glass

#### 0:06 - 0:10 | Transition Through Window
- **Movement**: Camera passes "through" window glass
- **Transition**: Smooth, seamless pass-through
- **Interior Reveal**: Beautiful, bright interior space
- **Lighting Inside**:
  - Natural light flooding in from windows
  - Bright, airy atmosphere
  - Soft shadows
  - NO interior lights on (morning daylight only)

#### 0:10 - 0:12 | Interior View
- **Final Frame**: Looking into living space
- **Details to Show**:
  - Open floor plan
  - Modern furniture (minimalist, neutral colors)
  - Hardwood floors or light tiles
  - White/cream walls
  - Natural textures (wood, stone, fabric)
  - Plants for organic touch
  - Bright, welcoming atmosphere

### Color Grading (Morning)
- **Temperature**: Warm (3200K-4500K)
- **Exposure**: Bright (+0.5 to +1 stop)
- **Highlights**: Preserved, not blown out
- **Shadows**: Lifted slightly
- **Saturation**: Natural, slightly enhanced
- **Look**: Fresh, clean, hopeful

## Video 2: Evening Scene (Dark Mode)

### Scene Description
**Setting**: Evening twilight, interior lights on

### Camera Movement Timeline

#### 0:00 - 0:03 | Opening Shot
- **View**: Same house exterior
- **Time**: Blue hour (20-30 min after sunset)
- **Lighting**: Deep blue sky, warm interior glow visible
- **Details to Show**:
  - Same architecture as morning
  - Windows glowing with warm interior light
  - Dark blue/purple sky (twilight)
  - Subtle exterior accent lighting
  - Warm light spilling onto ground from windows

#### 0:03 - 0:06 | Camera Move Toward Window
- **Movement**: Identical path to morning video
- **Speed**: Same timing for consistency
- **Focus**: Window glowing with warm interior light
- **Lighting**: Beautiful contrast between cool exterior and warm interior
- **Details**:
  - Window acts as light source
  - Warm glow becomes more prominent
  - Cooler ambient twilight on exterior

#### 0:06 - 0:10 | Transition Through Window
- **Movement**: Smooth pass-through window
- **Transition**: Into warmly lit interior
- **Interior Reveal**: Cozy, inviting evening atmosphere
- **Lighting Inside**:
  - Warm interior lights ON
  - Pendant lights, lamps, accent lighting
  - Warm white/amber temperature (2700K-3000K)
  - Soft, layered lighting
  - Fireplace or candles optional

#### 0:10 - 0:12 | Interior View
- **Final Frame**: Same angle as morning
- **Details to Show**:
  - Same room as morning video
  - Same furniture placement
  - Warm, intimate lighting
  - Multiple light sources visible
  - Cozy, relaxed atmosphere
  - Darker exterior visible through windows

### Color Grading (Evening)
- **Temperature**: Warm interior (2700K), cool exterior (6500K)
- **Exposure**: Balanced for interior (-0.3 to 0)
- **Highlights**: Warm lights can bloom slightly
- **Shadows**: Deep, rich blacks
- **Saturation**: Warm tones enhanced, cool tones subdued
- **Look**: Intimate, cozy, sophisticated

## Important Consistency Requirements

### MUST BE IDENTICAL BETWEEN VIDEOS
1. **Camera Path**: Exact same movement, speed, trajectory
2. **Framing**: Same composition and angles
3. **Architecture**: Same house, same windows
4. **Interior Layout**: Same room, same furniture positions
5. **Duration**: Same length for seamless switching
6. **Focal Length**: Same lens throughout

### KEY DIFFERENCES
1. **Time of Day**: Morning vs Evening
2. **Sky Color**: Blue/bright vs Blue hour/dark
3. **Interior Lighting**: Daylight only vs Artificial lights on
4. **Mood**: Bright/energetic vs Warm/intimate
5. **Color Temperature**: Cool morning vs Warm evening

## Production Methods

### Option 1: 3D Rendering (Recommended)
**Software**: Blender, Unreal Engine, Cinema 4D, V-Ray

**Advantages**:
- Perfect camera control
- Easy lighting adjustments
- Consistent between morning/evening
- Cost-effective
- Full control over every element

**Process**:
1. Model modern house exterior + interior
2. Set up camera path animation
3. Render morning lighting setup
4. Render evening lighting setup
5. Post-process and color grade

### Option 2: Real Footage
**Requirements**:
- Access to modern luxury home
- Professional camera equipment
- Drone or crane for smooth movement
- Shoot both times of day (morning + evening)
- VFX for "through window" transition

**Challenges**:
- Weather dependent
- Timing sensitive
- Expensive
- Harder to get perfect consistency

### Option 3: AI Video Generation
**Tools**: Runway Gen-3, Pika, Stability AI

**Advantages**:
- Fast production
- Cost-effective
- Creative flexibility

**Challenges**:
- Less control over consistency
- May need multiple attempts
- Quality varies

## Quality Checklist

### Before Final Export
- [ ] Both videos are exactly same duration
- [ ] Camera movements are identical
- [ ] Framing and composition match
- [ ] Architecture/interior layout consistent
- [ ] Smooth motion (no jitter)
- [ ] Proper color grading applied
- [ ] File sizes optimized for web
- [ ] Both MP4 and WebM formats exported
- [ ] Tested in browser with theme switching
- [ ] No audio tracks included
- [ ] Seamless loop capability (optional)
