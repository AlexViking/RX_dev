Our story

Built on craft, driven by design

RX Renovation was founded with a simple belief: every home deserves to be extraordinary. What began as a small design-build practice has grown into a full-service renovation studio trusted by homeowners across the greater Phoenix area.

We specialize in high-end residential renovations — kitchens, bathrooms, full-home transformations, and custom additions. Our process combines creative vision with technical precision, ensuring that every project is delivered on time, on budget, and beyond expectation.

Every project begins with listening. We learn how you live, what you love, and what you dream of. Then we build it.
